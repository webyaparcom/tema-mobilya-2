Our story

From one warehouse to three showrooms

Five stops over seventeen years.

  1. 2009

    warehouse

    The first showroom

    We showed our first room sets in a 300 m² warehouse in Sehitkamil.

  2. 2014

    chair

    Our own upholstery shop

    We began upholstering sofas and chairs in our own workshop.

  3. 2019

    storefront

    A second showroom

    A second store focused on dining and bedrooms opened in Sahinbey.

  4. 2023

    local_shipping

    Our own delivery team

    Delivery and assembly are now done by our own team, without subcontractors.

  5. 2026

    location_city

    The Nizip showroom

    A third showroom brought us closer to customers in the districts.
